# PhD Research

I completed my PhD at The Ohio State University under [Philip Schniter](https://ece.osu.edu/people/schniter.1), focusing on statistical signal processing, Bayesian inference, and deep learning for inverse problems.

[Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=lONr8RwAAAAJ&hl=en)

---

## Overview

During my PhD, I developed algorithms for solving linear and bilinear inverse problems based on the **Approximate Message Passing (AMP)** framework:

- **Bilinear Adaptive VAMP (BAd-VAMP)**: Proposed an algorithm combining VAMP with Expectation Maximization to solve bilinear inverse problems (e.g., dictionary learning, self-calibration). Robust to ill-conditioning of the forward operator.

- **MRI Image Reconstruction (DD-VAMP++)**: Proposed a plug-and-play recovery algorithm for MRI based on VAMP with novel damping and initialization schemes. Outperforms existing methods in convergence speed and accuracy on the fastMRI dataset with Cartesian sampling.
